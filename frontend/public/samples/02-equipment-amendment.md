# EQ-17 amendment A: Field-team assignments

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Not actual agency policy.

Effective: July 1, 2026. Owner: Operations desk.

## Field-team exception

For field-team assignments beginning on or after July 1, 2026, shared equipment
may be checked out for ten working days. This replaces the five-working-day
standard in EQ-17 only for those assignments. Other employees remain subject
to the five-working-day standard.

Field teams must record their assignment identifier and expected return date
in the equipment register. Extensions still require approval from the operations
desk before the return date.

## Approval record

The approving person's name and the approval meeting date are not included in
this copy. Consult the approval register to establish those facts.
